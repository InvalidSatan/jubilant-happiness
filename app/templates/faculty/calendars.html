Where to find a calendar ID
  1. Open Google Calendar and find the shop's calendar in the left sidebar.
  2. Hover it, click the three dots, and choose Settings and sharing.
  3. Scroll to Integrate calendar and copy the Calendar ID.
  4. Paste it into the row above and click Save.

Clearing the field disconnects that area and returns it to sample data. Shifts are read only — this app never changes anything in Google Calendar.
